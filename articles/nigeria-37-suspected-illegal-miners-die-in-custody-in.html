The fatalities have drawn attention to the risks faced by those detained in connection with artisanal mining activities and the broader challenges of enforcing mining regulations in the region.

The Nigerian Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC) confirmed it was conducting an investigation into the incident. A police statement said officers had been deployed to the area to assist with the inquiry and to determine what led to the deaths of the 37 individuals who were in custody on suspicion of illegal mining. The NSCDC attributed the deaths to an apparent cause that remains unspecified in the initial reports, though officials have not ruled out disease or other factors.
