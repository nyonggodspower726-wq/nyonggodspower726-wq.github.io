Residents from the South-South geopolitical zone residing in Kogi State have extended cash assistance to four students of the Federal University Lokoja, even as political rallies for President Bola Ahmed Tinubu and Governor Ahmed Usman Ododo intensify ahead of upcoming elections.

The cash relief, which was distributed to the students during a rally in the state capital, is part of a broader effort by the community to consolidate political support for the Tinubu-Ododo administration. According to reports, the beneficiaries were selected based on financial need, and the gesture was described as a "show of solidarity" with the president and the Kogi State governor.

The rally, which attracted thousands of supporters, featured speeches from party chieftains and government officials who praised the administration's policies and called for continued support. Governor Ododo, who is seeking re-election, used the occasion to highlight his administration's achievements and appeal to voters to maintain the status quo.

President Tinubu, although not physically present at the event, was represented by top party officials who reiterated the administration's commitment to youth empowerment and education. The cash relief to the FUL students is seen as a symbolic gesture of the president's "renewed hope" agenda, particularly in the education sector.

The South-South community in Kogi State has been an active supporter of the All Progressives Congress (APC) since the 2023 general elections. Their presence at the rally and the cash distribution underscores the growing political realignment in the state, where the APC is seeking to consolidate its hold ahead of the governorship poll. Political analysts say the move is part of a calculated effort to shore up support across geopolitical zones ahead of the election.

The beneficiaries of the cash relief expressed gratitude to the community, noting that the gesture came at a time when economic hardship has made it difficult for students to meet their academic needs. They also pledged their support for the Tinubu-Ododo administration, describing the president and the governor as leaders who understand the challenges facing Nigerian youths.

The rally and the cash relief distribution highlight the increasing role of grassroots support in Nigeria's political landscape, where community-based interventions are used to bolster political campaigns. As the governorship election approaches, similar gestures are expected across the state as parties jockey for position.
