Harry Kane extended his Bundesliga goalscoring lead with a hat-trick against Eintracht Frankfurt on Saturday, overtaking the league's all-time record in what served as a pointed response to Lamine Yamal's recent comments about the Ballon d'Or.

Harry Kane produced a clinical hat-trick as Bayern Munich secured a 4-1 victory over Eintracht Frankfurt on Saturday, a result that saw the England international surpass Gerd Müller's 60-year-old Bundesliga goalscoring record. The 30-year-old forward now has 215 goals in the German top flight, extending his lead at the summit of the scoring charts and reinforcing his status as one of Europe's most prolific strikers.

The record-breaking moment came in the 27th minute when Kane converted a penalty after being brought down in the box by Kevin Trapp. He added two further goals before half-time, completing his hat-trick with a close-range finish following a quick Bayern passing move. The performance marked Kane's 50th Bundesliga appearance goal and took his season tally to 28 goals across all competitions.

Kane's achievement arrives against the backdrop of recent comments from Barcelona teenager Lamine Yamal, who was quoted in Spanish media suggesting that young players are being overlooked for the Ballon d'Or in favour of established names. While Kane has been among the frontrunners for the 2026 Ballon d'Or, Yamal's remarks sparked discussion about whether the award's criteria are biased towards veteran players.

"It's a special feeling," Kane said after the match. "To break a record that stood for 60 years is something I never dreamed of as a kid. The boys deserve credit for getting me the ball in those positions."

Bayern coach Vincent Kompany praised his striker's consistency, noting that the record reflects Kane's dedication over multiple seasons in Germany. "Harry's numbers are incredible. He trains every day with the same intensity and that's why he's where he is," Kompany said.

The result moves Bayern to within three points of Bundesliga leaders Bayer Leverkusen, who drew 2-2 with Wolfsburg earlier on Saturday. Frankfurt remain in the lower half of the table, having conceded four goals for the second consecutive home game.

Kane's goalscoring feats have drawn comparisons with other European elite, but the England captain has remained focused on team objectives. "Individual records are nice but what matters most is the team's position," he said. "We're in a good position now and we need to keep pushing."

With the international break approaching, Kane will turn his attention to England's Nations League fixtures. The record-breaking performance provides a significant boost of confidence for both club and country as the season enters its decisive phase.

Bayern Munich will look to build on the victory when they face Augsburg next weekend, knowing that Kane's form could be the difference in a tight title race.
