THE NEWSROOM

About AI News Factory

AI News Factory is an independent digital newsroom built to turn developing stories, verified information and important context into clear, useful journalism.

Our mission

We want readers to understand not only what happened, but why it matters. Our newsroom combines source discovery, verification, corroboration, analysis and editorial review before stories are prepared for publication.

How we work

Stories begin with information from multiple news and information sources. The system compares reporting, identifies the central facts, looks for supporting evidence and develops an original editorial angle. Publication is subject to an editorial gate designed to reduce unsupported or low-value material.

Our approach

Automation helps us work faster, but speed is not the goal by itself. The goal is useful journalism: accurate, readable, contextual and transparent about uncertainty.

Independence

AI News Factory is not a substitute for the original reporting of source publications. We aim to add context and analysis rather than simply reproduce another publisher's work.
