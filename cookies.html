What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored by a browser. Similar technologies can also remember preferences or measure how a website is used.

Managing cookies

You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect certain site features.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, those services may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated when we introduce or remove services that use cookies.
