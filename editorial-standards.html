EDITORIAL

Editorial Standards

Our publishing process is designed around accuracy, corroboration, context and reader value.

Verification

Important claims should be supported by reliable sources. Where reporting conflicts or information remains uncertain, that uncertainty should be reflected rather than hidden.

Multiple-source thinking

We use multiple sources where available to identify common facts, differences in reporting and information that requires additional scrutiny.

Original value

Published stories should provide more than a mechanical rewrite. They should add context, explanation, synthesis or a useful editorial angle.

Corrections

If a material error is identified, we will review the affected story and correct or update it where appropriate.

AI assistance

AI systems may assist with research, organization, synthesis and drafting. Editorial responsibility remains with the publishing process, and automated output is not treated as inherently accurate.
